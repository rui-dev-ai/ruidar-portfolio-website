REFUND POLICY

Refund Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

Overview

This Refund Policy explains when refunds may or may not be issued for projects booked with Rui (Syed Ruidar Ali Shah). Because most work involves custom development, design, and strategy time, refund eligibility depends on how far a project has progressed.

Before Work Begins

If you've paid a deposit but work has not yet started, you may request a full refund, minus any payment processing fees already incurred.

After Work Has Started

Once work has begun, deposits are generally non-refundable, as they cover time, planning, and resources already committed to your project. Depending on how much work has been completed, a partial refund may be considered on a case-by-case basis.

After Project Completion

Once a project is delivered and approved by the client, refunds are not issued. If there's an issue with the delivered work, please raise it during the agreed revision period first — most issues can be resolved through revisions rather than a refund.

Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds will not be issued in the following cases:

  • Change of mind after work has already been completed as agreed
  • Delays caused by the client (e.g. late feedback, missing content/assets, delayed access to accounts)
  • Scope changes requested after work has started that were not part of the original agreement
  • Third-party costs already incurred on your behalf (e.g. app subscriptions, domain purchases, ad spend)

How to Request a Refund

If you believe you're eligible for a refund, email contactwithrui@gmail.com with your project details and the reason for the request. Refund requests are typically reviewed and responded to within 3–5 business days.

Disputes

If we can't reach an agreement directly, I'm open to discussing a fair resolution before any other steps are taken. Most disagreements can be worked out through direct, honest conversation.

This policy is provided for general informational purposes and does not constitute legal advice. Refund eligibility is ultimately assessed on a case-by-case basis, taking into account the specific circumstances of each project.
