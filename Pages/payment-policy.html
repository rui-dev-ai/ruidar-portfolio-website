PAYMENT POLICY

Payment Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

Overview

This Payment Policy outlines how payments are handled for projects booked through Rui (Syed Ruidar Ali Shah). It applies to all Shopify development, full-stack development, AI automation, digital marketing, and WordPress projects.

Payment Structure

Most projects follow one of these structures, agreed upon before work begins:

  • Deposit-based: A deposit (typically 50%) is due before work starts, with the remaining balance due upon project completion and before final delivery/handover.
  • Milestone-based: For larger projects, payment is split across agreed milestones (e.g. design approval, development completion, final delivery).

The exact structure for your project will be confirmed in writing (email or WhatsApp) before any work begins.

Accepted Payment Methods

Payment methods vary depending on your location and will be confirmed directly for your project — commonly this includes bank transfer, PayPal, Wise, or other mutually agreed methods for international clients.

Currency

Project pricing is typically quoted in USD for international clients unless otherwise agreed. Currency conversion fees, if any, are the responsibility of the client.

Late Payments

If a milestone or final payment is delayed beyond the agreed timeline, work on the project may be paused until payment is received. Continued delays may affect the project delivery timeline.

Invoicing

An invoice or payment confirmation will be provided for each payment made. Please keep these for your own records.

Taxes & Fees

Any applicable transaction fees, bank charges, or taxes related to sending payment are the responsibility of the client, unless otherwise agreed in writing.

Questions

For any payment-related questions before or during a project, reach out at contactwithrui@gmail.com or via WhatsApp.

This policy is provided for general informational purposes and does not constitute legal or financial advice. Specific payment terms for your project will always be confirmed directly with you in writing before work begins.
