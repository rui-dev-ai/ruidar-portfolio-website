ABOUT ME

From Shopify websites to intelligent systems.

I'm Syed Ruidar Ali Shah, known professionally as Rui. I started out as a Shopify developer and digital marketer — building stores and running Meta Ads for clients across the US, UK, and Europe, learning firsthand what actually turns visitors into buyers.

I lead a small team that helps me deliver full-stack and WordPress projects at scale, while I personally focus on Shopify architecture, AI-driven automation, and the strategy that ties it all together for growing businesses worldwide.
